# Frontier Labs Index

A curated index of the most influential AI research papers from seven frontier labs.

**Live site:** https://qy6on.github.io/frontier-labs-index/

## What it is

Frontier Labs Index brings together 59 landmark papers from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, NVIDIA, DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, and Zhipu AI, from Attention Is All You Need (2017) to DeepSeek-V4 and Kimi K3 (2026).

Each paper includes a short summary, its authors, year, and topic, plus a direct link to the paper itself: the arXiv record, the publisher DOI, or the page that hosts it.

## Explore

- **Search** titles, topics, labs, and authors
- **Filter** by lab, topic, and year
- **Sort** by newest, oldest, lab, or A to Z
- **Open any paper** for its summary, authors, and direct full-text link

## Coverage

- 59 papers
- 7 labs
- 11 topics, including Language models, Reasoning, Agents, Safety and alignment, Interpretability, and Audio
- 2017 to 2026

## About the links

Every link goes to the paper itself, not to a search page. That matters: some landmark papers, such as AlphaFold, have no arXiv version at all, and a title search for others returns unrelated results.

Summaries are written for this index and are not the authors' abstracts. Each paper belongs to its authors and publishers.
